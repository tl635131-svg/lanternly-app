Self-hosted fonts. All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL); each family's licence is in
<Family>-OFL.txt next to its files. None of them declares a Reserved Font Name, so the subset (*-latin.woff2,
NotoSansTamil-tamil.woff2) and pinned-width variants may keep their names.

Source: Google Fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts, ofl/<family>/).
- Bricolage Grotesque: BricolageGrotesque.ttf, BricolageGrotesque-latin.woff2 (Latin subset)
- DM Sans: DMSans.ttf, DMSans-latin.woff2 (Latin subset)
- Fraunces: Fraunces.ttf, Fraunces-latin.woff2 (Latin subset)
- Inter: Inter.ttf, Inter-latin.woff2 (Latin subset)
- Manrope: Manrope.ttf, Manrope-latin.woff2 (Latin subset)
- Instrument Serif: InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf
- Noto Sans Tamil: NotoSansTamil-tamil.woff2 (Tamil block U+0B80-0BFF, wght 100-900, width pinned to 100)
